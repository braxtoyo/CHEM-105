CATEGORY 6: MATTER WAVES AND UNCERTAINTY

MATTER WAVES
Moving matter has wave properties.
Use the de Broglie equation on the equation sheet.
Wavelength decreases as momentum increases.
At fixed speed, greater mass gives shorter wavelength.
At fixed mass, greater speed gives shorter wavelength.
A large everyday object has an extremely short wavelength, making its wave behavior difficult to notice.
Use kilograms and meters per second.

UNCERTAINTY
Position uncertainty describes how precisely position is specified.
Momentum uncertainty describes how precisely momentum is specified.
Smaller position uncertainty requires larger momentum uncertainty.
The uncertainty principle is a fundamental limit, not merely a consequence of bad equipment.
For fixed mass, momentum uncertainty can be related to speed uncertainty.
Use the uncertainty equation on the equation sheet.
If asked for the minimum uncertainty, use equality at the limit.
